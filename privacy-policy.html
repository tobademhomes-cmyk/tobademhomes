Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

At Tobadem Homes, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy and protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, interact with our property listings, or contact us regarding real estate purchases and land banking opportunities.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when making inquiries or using our platform, including:

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, WhatsApp contact details, and email address when you reach out for property inquiries or request documentation.
  • Property Inquiry Details: Information regarding your preferred property locations, budget, and specific investment preferences.
  • Technical Data: Essential session cookies, browser type, and IP address necessary for website functionality and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide you with seamless real estate services, including:

  • Responding promptly to your inquiries via phone, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Providing verified property titles, allocation details, and due diligence reports.
  • Managing administrative authentication and security through our database infrastructure (Supabase).
  • Improving site functionality, security, and user experience.

3. Important Notice on Payments & Financial Data

Strict Corporate Account Payment Policy: Tobadem Homes operates under a strict operational policy. All financial payments for property acquisitions or land allocations must be made directly into designated official corporate accounts. We do not collect cash, nor do we permit payments into personal accounts of sales consultants or third parties.

We do not collect or store full credit card details or direct bank account numbers on this website.

4. Third-Party Services & Integrations

To deliver an efficient digital experience, our website interacts with reputable third-party infrastructure:

  • Supabase: Serves as our secure cloud backend provider for database storage and administrative access security.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): Used for direct instant communication between clients and our customer care team.
  • Hosting Providers: Secure hosting environments (such as Vercel) to deliver fast and reliable web access.

5. Cookies and Web Storage

We use essential functional cookies and session tokens to ensure website security and proper portal functionality. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or browsing data to third-party advertisers.

6. Data Protection & Compliance

Tobadem Homes adheres to applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023). We implement strict administrative and technical measures (including Row Level Security) to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or alteration of data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of your contact details.
  • Request deletion of your inquiry records from our communication systems.
