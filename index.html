About Us

Delivering excellence, integrity, and maximum capital appreciation in property acquisition.

At Tobadem Homes, we deal exclusively in verified properties for sale and strategic land banking investments. Our core mission is to help smart investors, property buyers, and Diaspora clients secure legitimate titles, beat market inflation, and build generational wealth across Nigeria. Every year, thousands of hardworking Nigerians and Diaspora investors lose millions of Naira to real estate scams, double-allocated plots, government-encumbered lands, and unverified titles. Seeing buyers burned simply because proper due diligence wasn't performed is the exact problem TOBADEM HOMES was established to solve.

We exist to eliminate the anxiety, risk, and second-guessing from property acquisition. Dealing exclusively in verified land banking opportunities and real estate sales across Lagos, Ogun State, Ibadan, Abuja, and nationwide, every plot in our portfolio undergoes exhaustive legal title verification, survey checks, and risk assessments before it reaches the market.

Real estate shouldn't be a gamble. We bridge the gap between smart investors and 100% secure, high-yield land assets so you can grow your wealth with complete peace of mind.

Meet Our Founder & CEO — Adelugbin Michael Tobi

"In cybersecurity, the principle is simple: zero trust—always verify. We brought that exact mindset into real estate."

Adelugbin Michael Tobi built his career in cybersecurity around one unyielding rule: never assume, always verify. After watching hard-working families and diaspora investors lose their savings to unverified land titles, he realized Nigerian real estate needed that exact system of digital defense.

As a Product Designer, Cybersecurity Analyst, and CSEAN Associate Member, Michael merged high-tech risk analysis with rigorous legal due diligence to found TOBADEM HOMES. Today, he leads a high-performing team dedicated to eliminating real estate anxiety—ensuring every property in our portfolio is 100% vetted, secure, and built to create generational wealth.
